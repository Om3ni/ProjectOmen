# Privacy Policy for Project Omen

**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026

## Overview

Project Omen is a free, offline desktop application designed for editing and managing game data files. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data.**

- All files and data you work with remain entirely on your local device
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind is performed
- No user accounts, registration, or login systems are required
- No network connections are made to our servers or any third-party services

## Local Data Storage

Project Omen uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) solely to:
- Auto-save your work locally to prevent data loss
- Remember your editor preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings or by using the "New File" feature in the application.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this privacy policy and notify users through the application or our distribution channels.
