=== SYSTEM DESIGN HIGHLIGHTS ===

Combat System:
- Uses Action/Reaction mechanics
- Multiple damage types (Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, etc.)
- Status effects that modify combat effectiveness
- Healing and support abilities
- Crowd control (stuns, roots, grapples)

Social & Utility:
- Awareness (perception checks)
- Stealth mechanics
- Speechcraft (persuasion/deception)
- Lockpicking system
- Pickpocketing mechanics

Crafting & Professions:
- Fishing (with experience tracking)
- Mining (ore gathering)
- Cooking (food preparation)
- Blacksmithing (weapon/armor crafting)
- Alchemy (potion making)
- Enchanting (magical enhancements)

Special Systems:
- Casino mechanics for gambling
- Teaching system where Scholars can train others
- Sparring system with grappling mechanics
- Multiple resource types (Health Pool, Mana Pool, Energy Pool, Action Points, Reaction Points)

=== IMPLEMENTATION NOTES ===

Action/Reaction System:
- Standard actions cost Action Points (Resource 3)
- Reactions cost Reaction Points (Resource 4)
- Reactions trigger in response to specific events (attacks, spells, etc.)
- Typical setup: 2 actions, 1 reaction per turn

Resource Management:
- Health Pool (0): Primary hit points
- Mana Pool (1): Magic spells cost mana
- Energy Pool (2): Physical abilities cost energy
- Action Points (3): Actions per turn
- Reaction Points (4): Reactions per turn

Profession Experience System:
- Each profession tracks experience separately (Attributes 16-21)
- Abilities grant experience via ExpGain field
- Pattern: ExpGain = attributeId,amount
- Example: ExpGain = 16,10 gives 10 fishing experience

Status Effect Categories:
- Crowd Control: Grappled (0), Stunned (1), Root, Silence, Blind
- Damage Over Time: Bleeding (2), Poisoned (3), Burning (4)
- Buffs: Blessed (7), Defending (10), Invisible (9)
- Debuffs: Cursed (8), Frozen (5), Shocked (6)
- Special: Concentrating (11), Teaching (13), Learning (14), Drunk (12)

Combat Role Distribution:
- Tank: Defensive Stance, Shield Block, Taunt abilities
- DPS: High damage abilities across Melee/Range/Magic
- Healer: Heal, Mass Heal, Resurrection, status cleansing
- Support: Buffs, debuffs, crowd control, utility

Special Mechanics:
- Teaching: Scholar casts "Teach Combat/Magic/Skills" on target
- Learning: Target gains Learning status, receives knowledge transfer
- Sparring: Non-lethal combat with Yield option
- Casino: Gambling mechanics using dice rolls vs DC
- Grappling: Status effect preventing movement/actions

Damage Types Represented:
- Physical (standard melee/ranged)
- Fire, Cold, Lightning (elemental)
- Holy (divine/radiant)
- Shadow (necrotic/dark)
- Psychic (mental/mind)
- Magic (arcane/force)
- Nonlethal (sparring)

Advanced Ability Patterns:
- TriggerOnKill: Cleave activates after killing blow
- TriggerOnLowHealth: Berserk activates when health is low
- RequiresFlank: Backstab needs flanking position
- LowHealthOnly: Execute mechanics for finishing
- Contagious: Plague spreads to nearby targets
